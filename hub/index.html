Who's behind this

I'm not a lawyer.
That's the point.

For more than three years I studied real discrimination cases, one at a time, on my own. I wasn't looking for the law. I was looking for the pattern: why some people with real situations got nowhere, and others got taken seriously.

I studied history the same way, strategically. How change actually happens. How the smaller side, the one with less power, still finds a way to win.

And I kept seeing the same thing. Something wrong would happen to someone, and they'd feel it, and they'd have no words for it and no idea what to do. So they did nothing, and it happened again. Civic Armor exists to fix that: power and how it works, turned into something a regular person can use.

“I'm not here to be a hero. I'm just trying to be the change I wish existed when I watched people get hurt and stay stuck.”

What I am

  • Someone who studied the pattern for years, until I couldn't let it go
  • The person who helps you see what happened before you ever walk into a lawyer's office
  • The writer of a method, a novel and a game that all teach the same thing

What I'm not

  • A lawyer. Nothing here is legal advice.
  • Someone who'll tell you what your case is worth
  • A hero. The block does this together.

Being honest about that line is the first thing I'll ask you to trust me on. — Lamont

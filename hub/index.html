Who's behind this

I'm Lamont Ragland II. I'm not a lawyer, and nothing here is legal advice. For more than three years I studied real discrimination cases, one at a time, looking for what the people who got taken seriously had in common. I studied history the same way: how the smaller side, the one with less power, still finds a way to win.

The pattern turned into a method one regular person can use: see it, document it, judge it honestly, and walk into a lawyer's office prepared. The novel and the game teach the same thing to everybody else at the table.

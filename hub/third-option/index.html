Something happened at work. You can't stop thinking about it.

Passed over for something you earned. Write-ups out of nowhere. The sudden chill after you spoke up. Most people feel like they have two options.

Option one

Swallow it

Keep your head down and hope it stops. It usually happens again.

Option two

Blow up

Say what you feel, and hand them the reason they were looking for.

The third option

Find out

See it clearly, build the record, judge it honestly, and walk in prepared.

What's inside

A working instrument, not a shelf book

Every tool in it is something you'll actually do, with your own situation in mind. Take your time. Stop when you need to. Come back.

  1. See itWhat actually counts as discrimination: because of who you are, a real harm, and a connection between the two.
  2. Prove itThe ladder of evidence. Turn a bad feeling and a spoken moment into a pattern of small, dated, real pieces that point one way.
  3. Judge itAn honest read of your own case, strengths and weaknesses both. The people who do well saw their case clearly first.
  4. File itThe multi-claim map, and the deadlines you cannot miss.
  5. After you fileWhat happens next, so the waiting isn't the scary part.
  6. One case, start to finishEverything you built, in one place.

What's inside

Not a book you read once and shelve. A working instrument: every tool in it is something you'll actually do, with your own situation in mind.

  1. See itWhat actually counts as discrimination: because of who you are, a real harm, and a connection between the two.
  2. Prove itThe ladder of evidence. Turn a bad feeling and a spoken moment into a pattern of small, dated, real pieces that point one way.
  3. Judge itAn honest read of your own case, strengths and weaknesses both. The people who do well saw their case clearly first.
  4. File itThe multi-claim map, and the deadlines you cannot miss.
  5. After you fileWhat happens next, so the waiting isn't the scary part.
  6. One case, start to finishFollow Renée from a bad feeling to a prepared case, the whole way through.
Educational only, not legal advice. This book helps you understand, document and assess what might be happening to you. It doesn't create an attorney-client relationship and isn't a substitute for a licensed attorney in your state. It helps you see and prepare; a lawyer confirms and acts.

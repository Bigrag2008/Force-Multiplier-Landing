# The Second Number — Spanish glossary (Glosario)

One word per concept, everywhere: cards, website, log, rulebook. Neutral US/Latin American Spanish, the way it's spoken in Dayton, Chicago, Houston or LA: clear, warm, a little street, never stiff. Address players as **tú** on cards and screens; the table as **ustedes**. Keep sentences short (the English is short on purpose). Keep numbers, dollar amounts and percentages exactly as in English ($1,000 stays $1,000; 391% stays 391%).

**Never translate:** people's names (Marcus, Nia, Simone, Zay, Dre, Terrell), real places and organizations (Gem City Market, Dunbar High School, Wilberforce University, Central State University, Sinclair, Good Samaritan Hospital, Five Rivers MetroParks, Adventure Central, NCR, GM), laws and court cases by name (Fair Housing Act, Shelley v. Kraemer, Voting Rights Act) — you may add a short gloss in parentheses the first time on a card if needed.

## Voice
- Fronts are the seller's pitch: keep the sales energy ("¡Aprobado!", "¡Múdate hoy!").
- Red / amber / blue are plain and sharp, one sentence each.
- The game never shames anyone for signing; it shows how the trap works.
- Keep `{slots}` exactly as written (e.g. `{n}`, `{name}`, `${a}`, `${total}`) and keep the same slots in the Spanish.

Subject: The three glasses

Hey,

In the game, you read every deal through three colored lenses. They work on real paperwork too:

🔴 **Red, the cost.** What does this really cost, all in, over time? Not the payment: the total.

🟡 **Amber, the clock.** How fast do they want you to sign? "Today only" and "sign now" are the trap telling on itself. Real help can wait a day.

🔵 **Blue, the connection.** Who's on the other side, and who are they connected to? The store and the lender are often the same company.

Next time somebody slides a paper across a table at you, put on all three before you pick up the pen.
